    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. Every room, every player. If you love it, you can buy us a coffee.

    Do we need an app or an account?

    No. It runs in your phone's browser. You just type a name so your team knows who's who.

    How many can play?

    1 to 8 is the sweet spot. Big family? Split into two teams and race.

    Is it OK for kids?

    Yes. Made for ages 8 to 80. The spooky rooms are friendly-spooky.

    What do you store about us?

    Almost nothing. The nicknames and team name you type, your room's progress, and leaderboard times. No emails, no phone numbers, no tracking. Rooms delete themselves after a week.
